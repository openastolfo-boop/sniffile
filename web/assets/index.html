about sniffile

A local file search. It indexes names, paths, dates and the text inside plain-text files. Optional: point it at a local ollama and it also reads photo contents and translates queries into English keywords.

Nothing leaves the machine. No accounts, no telemetry, no cloud, no "sign in to search your own disk".

honest limits

  • Keyword matching, not vector embeddings. A photo is only findable by name or caption.
  • EXIF orientation is ignored; some phone photos may look sideways in the preview.
  • Only plain text is read for content, not PDF/DOCX.
  • Hidden folders and node_modules-style junk are skipped.
